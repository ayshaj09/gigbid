# Technical write-up outline (1,200-1,800 words, for LinkedIn / a blog)

Write this yourself, in your own voice. Suggested shape:
1. The problem in two sentences (freelancers' time vs generic AI proposals).
2. One design decision that surprised you, with the measurement that settled it (e.g. which
   retrieval variant won, and by how much, on which jobs).
3. Grounding: how claim verification works and one before/after example.
4. Human in the loop: why nothing is sent automatically, and how durable approvals work.
5. A failure you found and fixed (from your taxonomy).
6. What you would build next. Link to the repo and demo video.
Avoid: claims you did not measure, screenshots with real client data, the reviewer login.
